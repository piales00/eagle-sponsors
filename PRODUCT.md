# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS, single page deployed on Vercel (eagle-sponsors.vercel.app). Sponsor edition of eagle-junction-2026, which stays as the UTP-facing version.

## Users

Primary: companies and brands (marketing, employer branding, tech/cloud vendors, Peruvian corporates) deciding whether to sponsor the team's trip from Lima to Junction 2026 in Espoo, Finland. Secondary: the team's LinkedIn network who share the page.

## Product Purpose

Announce team Eagle's run at Junction 2026 (13–15 Nov 2026, Hype Areena, Espoo; ~2,000 builders, 48 h, 100+ countries, €100k prize pool) and sign sponsors for the trip through three tiers (Aliado, Socio, Principal) or in-kind support.

## Positioning

A UTP team with production and hackathon track record (not first-timers) taking a sponsor's brand onto the stage that Google DeepMind, NVIDIA, OpenAI and AWS sponsor.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Bilingual ES/EN.
- Team name: Eagle. Members 02–04 are not confirmed yet; placeholders must be labelled as such.
- Acceptance to Junction is not confirmed in the evidence; copy must not claim the team was accepted.
- Junction partner logos may be shown only as event partners, with a visible note that they do not sponsor the team.

## Brand Commitments

Visual essence pinned by the user: the Junction 2026 site (Renaissance paintings remixed with hacker culture, computer-vision detection boxes, dark ground, manifesto voice "move the center of gravity"). Junction's own images are not reused; public-domain originals get the same treatment.

Photo: ~/Downloads hacker pass. No sponsor commitments exist yet; none may be invented. Tiers carry no prices; amounts go in the deck sent by email.

## Product Principles

1. The ask (back the trip) is visible in the first viewport.
2. The sponsor sees an empty slot for its logo next to the event's partners, and how big it gets per tier.
3. Proof over adjectives: shipped work with real numbers.
4. The team, not one person.
